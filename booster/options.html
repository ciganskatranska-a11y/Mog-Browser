Engine

Direct boosts the page's own player: fullscreen keeps working and there's no delay. Capture grabs the whole tab's sound and works everywhere, including protected video. Auto uses Direct when it can. If a site goes quiet in Direct mode, click Direct in the popup to switch that tab to Capture.

Auto-mog and Speed-maxx in embedded players need access to all sites. Chrome will ask once.

          Outside-the-browser maxxing

          The extension can only push what Chrome sends to your device. These get the rest.

          • Set your system volume and the site's own player volume to 100%. The booster multiplies what the page outputs.
          • Windows: Settings, System, Sound, your output device, then turn on Loudness Equalization or Audio enhancements if your driver offers it.
          • Windows: in the classic Sound control panel, Communications tab, choose "Do nothing" so calls don't duck your audio.
          • Small speakers waste power on bass they can't play. Use the Phone speaker cope stack: low cut plus Phantom bass.
          • Loud for long is how hearing goes. Above about 85 dB, keep sessions short.
